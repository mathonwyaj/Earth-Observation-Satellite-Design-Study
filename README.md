# Earth Observation Satellite Design Study

I compared three camera payloads against a 150 kg spacecraft mass limit using Python, COMET and Unity.

At a solar incidence angle of 25°, reducing the 140 W camera from 35 kg to 32 kg brought the spacecraft down to 149.50 kg, leaving 0.50 kg of margin. At 45°, the larger solar array needed for the same power demand pushed the design to 151.00 kg.
